TimbSwap · pre-round segment betting

Load all six tokens, then play.

Hold an active Compete ticket and the round hands you six segment tokens — one per segment, unique to you, untradeable, redeemed every round. First load every token with a TIMBS chip (min 5): all six carry a stake before the board opens. Then place them — pick a spot, read exactly what it calls for, set it down. Once it's down, it's final. Tokens on the board are collected with their chips for distribution; tokens you load but never place keep their TIMBS with you.

    Every placement needs an approval before it lands — and once approved, the placement and its stake can't be undone. The live table shows every bet and amount the moment it's approved, but identity stays masked: you see a suit and a short tag, never who. Seating isn't settled yet — for now players are auto-grouped four to a table. The lower rows are group bets — Vowels and Your Ticket, then Red / Black (even money, read off the pocket colours — 18 characters each). Only even / odd are gone, since a letter-and-number alphabet has no parity. Payout is pari-mutuel: every placed chip at the table forms one pot, and when the round draws its six locked characters, the pot (minus rake) is split among the winners in proportion to stake × fair-multiple — so a longshot that hits earns far more per chip than a favourite. The board's 36 ÷ (winning symbols) − 1 odds are the relative weights, not a fixed house payout; the actual return floats with how the table bet. Rake is 0% for now — that's the house-edge dial you turn up later. Losing chips fund the winners; nobody banks against the table.
